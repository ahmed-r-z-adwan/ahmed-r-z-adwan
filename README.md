## Ahmed Adwan

Final-year student in **Mobile Computing and Smart Device Applications** at the Islamic University of Gaza, graduating January 2027.

I build Android apps for conditions where the network is the unreliable part. That is not a hypothetical constraint where I work — it decides the architecture. Local storage is the source of truth and sync is something that happens later, to data that is already safe. What should happen when two devices disagree about the same record is the problem I am working through at the moment.

I also prefer measuring a claim to asserting it. Where a project here claims an optimisation or a guarantee, there is a test in the repository that produces the number or proves the claim.
